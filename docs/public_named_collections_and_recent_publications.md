# Named collections and new publications

Requested 2 November 2026.

The review selector places Weekly discovery first and Corpus relevance audit second, followed by APWG eCrime, Bellingcat, Consensus, FIRST, M3AAWG, SANS, SOUPS and Virus Bulletin. Source manifests use the existing console, global buffer, mechanical gates, classifier transport and publication rules. Relocated undecided records retain their IDs, content hashes, reservations and decisions.

Public named collections follow explicit collection-source provenance, retaining original authors and publishers. FIRST, M3AAWG, Bellingcat, Consensus and SANS have profiles. Every configured named collection has a stable public page, even before papers are accepted. Empty pages state that no papers have been accepted yet and link to the original archive. Only accepted records appear in each bibliography. Homepage and research-hub source links are alphabetical. The homepage link list is regenerated from the accepted snapshot during each Pages build, rather than hard-coded to APWG and SOUPS.

The New publications button immediately precedes the named collections. Its collection uses verified full publication dates within the preceding90days, inclusive of the90-day cutoff and today in America/Toronto. First online publication takes precedence over a later print issue. Revisions, metadata deposits, discovery, harvesting, ALEX acceptance and listing dates do not restart that window. Year-only and month-only evidence is never expanded into an invented day. A coarse earlier primary publication date prevents substituting a later complete print date. Future scheduled dates are excluded.

The initial bounded Crossref pass supplies175 records with exact publisher-reported dates, source URLs, observed titles, date fields and check timestamps in config/publication_dates.json. Title and DOI reconciliation precede use. Other records remain outside this collection until date evidence is verified; the page states this limitation. Collection membership does not change acceptance or quality decisions. Crossref harvest now retains verified date fields for future accepted publications, and the publisher preserves that optional metadata.

The full bibliography is rendered without JavaScript. A small progressive enhancement ages records out against the current Toronto date when the page is opened between deployments. It never imports records or uses retrieval dates. The original public JSON/CSV downloads remain unchanged during artifact staging; the collection enriches only its in-memory display from the date-evidence registry.

Validation covers alphabetical source links, stable empty collection pages, original attribution,90-day boundaries, future dates, partial dates, older online versus newer print dates, creation/indexing/update exclusion, additive enrichment, generated-page reachability, SEO, schema and unchanged downloads. Synthetic examples stay in isolated tests. No unreviewed record is added to the public corpus.
