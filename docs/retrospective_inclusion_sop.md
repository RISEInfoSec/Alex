# ALEX Retrospective Inclusion SOP

Status: approved operating policy.
Effective date: 1 November 2026.
Approved by: Neil Schwartzman, President and Founder, RISE Information Security Foundation.
Scope: all ALEX discovery, editorial review and source collections, including regular weekly intake, SOUPS, Virus Bulletin and APWG eCrime.

## Purpose

Prioritize professional development in current OSINT and cybercrime investigation while preserving research with demonstrable continuing or historical value. Age informs processing priority and contextual notices; it is not proof of obsolescence, a numerical score penalty or an automatic rejection rule.

## Routine window and older exceptions

Use the current publication year and four preceding publication years for routine discovery. In 2026 this is 2022–2026; advance the window each January using America/Toronto. Retain original publication dates, separately from conference, data-collection, discovery and retrieval dates.

For the preceding five publication years (2017–2021 in 2026), process older work selectively when it offers demonstrable continuing value. For earlier work (2016 and before), require a named foundational or continuing-value exception. Do not routinely exhaust decades of archives.

After required mechanical checks, record the current investigative or research application, material actually inspected and assessment depth, later work checked, continuing contribution, obsolete dependencies or limitations, reviewer/date and content identity. Explain why the item remains useful alongside more recent work. Preserve uncertainty when supersession cannot be established.

Approximately 80% of substantive preparation effort should initially serve the routine window and 20% documented older exceptions. This is a scheduling guide, not a source quota, acceptance target or reason to delay cleared work. Respect existing authorized release and weekly-backlog priorities.

Tool, platform, API, browser-interface and live-indicator guidance requires verification against current conditions even within the routine window. Prioritize the newest 24 months for such guidance, and recheck at least annually and after known changes. Methods, evidence reasoning and behavioral findings may retain value longer. Treat historical indicators as dated observations.

## Public presentation

Older work with verified continuing value may remain in default professional-development results and must carry the older-material caveat. Foundational usefulness does not exempt a record from that notice.

Material included primarily for historical context belongs in an optional public historical view, excluded from default professional-development results. Give every such record a compact H indicator in list view and a visible Historical reference label in the paper detail view. Include an H = Historical reference key above the list. Pair the indicator with text/accessibility labels so color is not the sole cue. Show its original publication year and explain its historical contribution. Do not infer that every older item is historical-only.

Place the full neutral notice in the paper drill-down/detail view, not in each list row. Use a compact, separate 5+ or earlier-research marker in list view for older continuing-value work, with a key above the list; H is reserved for historical-only material and must not be assigned solely by age. Keep the existing navy/slate design and restrained accent colors.

All material older than five years must display, in its detail view, a visible, neutral caveat that later research or developments may have refined, qualified or superseded findings or practical guidance. Do not assert that a particular finding has been superseded without evidence. Display original publication year beside the notice and retain author attribution.

Use the exact fifth-anniversary boundary when a full publication date is available. Where only a year is known, conservatively show the caveat from publication_year <= current_year - 5, without inventing a month/day or claiming an exact elapsed age. For unknown dates, mark age unverified and establish the date before presenting the resource as current. Recompute the flag with time; a reviewed continuing-value exception retains its caveat.

Known supersession at any age receives a specific, evidence-backed explanation and a newer reference when verified. An age caveat is not a substitute for identifying known errors, obsolete versions or superseding evidence.

## Proposed public wording

The requirement for an age caveat is approved. The following wording is a draft for editorial review, not an assertion that public UI implementation is complete.

Badge: Earlier research · [publication year]

This work reflects the evidence and technical environment at the time of publication. Its methods and insights may remain valuable, while some findings or practical guidance may have been refined or superseded by subsequent research and developments. Please consult more recent sources before applying it to current research or investigations.

Historical-only badge: Historical reference · [publication year]

Additional historical-view context: Included for its historical contribution. Technical details and recommendations should be read in the context of the period in which the work was published.

## APWG release timing

Preserve the existing annual December check for that year's APWG eCrime papers. The owner confirmed that APWG publishes at the end of November; classify the 2026 papers as awaiting scheduled publication until the December intake check. Accepted-paper announcements are not evidence that the final papers are already available, and their absence from full-paper intake before publication is not an overdue-discovery failure. Keep abstract/preprint and final published versions distinct and follow the existing availability, identity and inclusion checks.

## Existing records, decisions and implementation

Preserve published records, original identities, content hashes, prior approvals, exclusions and all quality/material/access gates. Defer lower-priority historical recovery explicitly; do not delete discoveries, reverse editorial decisions or introduce numerical age penalties.

Existing-publication visibility changes require a documented classification of continuing-value versus historical-only material, with a preview and verification of labels, default search, explicit historical search and direct links. This SOP authorizes the policy and its historical-view requirement; do not claim badges, filtering or a corpus migration are live until implemented and verified. The requested notice wording remains a draft pending editorial acceptance.

Every worker pass must read this SOP with the workflow and material policies. It supersedes earlier instructions to exhaust the complete historical archive where those instructions conflict with the five-year routine window. Preserve archive checkpoints and mark deferred older scope honestly. Discoveries, mechanically held candidates, editorial approvals, releases and public verification remain separate states. Ready manual batches may contain 1–20 items; do not wait to fill 20.
