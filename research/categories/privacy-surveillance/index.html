Privacy & Surveillance research

128 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Privacy and surveillance research examines how information about people is collected, connected and used, and what consequences those practices can have. It can help investigators recognise the exposure created by data collection and the limits of an inference about an individual.

Scope of this collection

Papers in this bibliography carry ALEX’s Privacy & Surveillance research-category assignment. The subject can intersect with social platforms, identification methods and information governance. The listing preserves those existing assignments without making a legal judgement about a particular investigative practice.

Using this research

Examine which people and data sources a study represents, what consent or access assumptions it makes and how it evaluates potential harm. Technical feasibility does not determine legal authority. Use appropriate current policy and legal guidance separately when planning the collection or use of personal information.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
