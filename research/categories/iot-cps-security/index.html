Internet of Things and cyber-physical systems research concerns connected devices and systems that interact with a physical environment. The relationship between a digital observation and a physical event depends on device behaviour, system design and the available records.

Scope of this collection

This bibliography uses the IoT & CPS Security research-category assignment. It can include device, network and detection research associated with these environments. The category does not establish that a method tested on one device or testbed applies to another operational setting.

Using this research

Compare device capabilities, firmware, communication protocols and testbed assumptions. Look for the distinction between simulated traffic and observations from operational systems. Use the original paper to understand what a method measures and which safety or environmental conditions constrained its evaluation.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
