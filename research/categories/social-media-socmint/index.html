Social Media & SOCMINT research

65 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Social-media intelligence examines information shared through online platforms and communities. Posts, accounts and interactions can provide investigative leads, but their meaning depends on platform context, collection conditions and the reliability of the material being observed.

Scope of this collection

The Social Media & SOCMINT category groups papers about social-platform information and its analysis. Subject matter can include public-post collection, community behaviour, information extraction and cyber-threat awareness. The separate Social Media Analysis investigation collection provides another route through application-oriented assignments.

Using this research

Compare how studies select accounts or posts, handle missing or deleted content and establish the context of an interaction. A public post, an account identity and the person operating it are separate evidential questions. Read the underlying study before interpreting a platform-derived indicator as a finding.
