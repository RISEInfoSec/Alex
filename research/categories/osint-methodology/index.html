OSINT Methodology research

60 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) turns publicly available information into material that can support an investigation. The methodological questions matter as much as the tools: how information was found, whether it can be verified, and what conclusions the available evidence can support.

Scope of this collection

This collection contains papers assigned to ALEX’s OSINT Methodology research category. It brings together work on information collection, validation, linkage and the organisation of investigative work. It is a subject collection, rather than a list of papers selected for one particular kind of investigation.

Using this research

Use the bibliography to compare collection approaches, examine validation methods and trace the research behind an investigative technique. Consult the original paper for its data, assumptions and limitations before applying a method. A classification in this collection does not establish that a tool is suitable for a particular case.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
