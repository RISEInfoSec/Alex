Digital Forensics & Evidence research

94 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Digital forensics examines artefacts and the processes used to collect, preserve and interpret them. A research method can illuminate what an artefact may show while also exposing dependencies on the device, software version or circumstances in which it was produced.

Scope of this collection

This collection uses the Digital Forensics & Evidence research-category assignment. It brings together papers associated with digital artefacts, evidence processing and forensic methods. The Forensic Investigation collection groups papers by investigative application rather than by subject alone.

Using this research

Compare the steps needed to reproduce a method, the environment in which it was tested and the limits of the inference it supports. Preserve the distinction between an observed artefact and an interpretation of it. Consult applicable procedures and the original research before using a technique in casework.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
