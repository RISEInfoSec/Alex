Cybercrime & Fraud research

284 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Cybercrime and fraud research examines offending that involves digital systems, online deception and the people or organisations affected. It provides ways to frame investigative questions about a scheme, its participants and the evidence needed to understand it.

Scope of this collection

This subject collection contains papers assigned to Cybercrime & Fraud. Its scope is broader than one mechanism such as phishing and can include research on offending patterns, online markets, victimisation and fraud-related behaviour represented in the accepted corpus.

Using this research

Use the literature to compare explanations of a scheme and identify the evidence a study actually used. Distinguish reported victim experiences, observed transactions and inferred offender behaviour. Population estimates and findings from a particular platform or jurisdiction should be read within their stated sampling limits.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
