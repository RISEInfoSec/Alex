Network & Infrastructure Security research

194 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Network and infrastructure security research studies the systems that carry communications and support digital services. Investigative interpretation depends on where an observation was made and what the monitoring system could see.

Scope of this collection

ALEX’s Network & Infrastructure Security category determines this bibliography. It groups subject-oriented work concerning communications, network monitoring and infrastructure protection. Network Investigation and Incident Response provide separate collections organised around investigative use.

Using this research

Check the topology, traffic source and observation point used in an evaluation. Encrypted, sampled or incomplete traffic can change what a result means. A reported detection rate or network association should be interpreted against the conditions and ground truth documented by the original study.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
