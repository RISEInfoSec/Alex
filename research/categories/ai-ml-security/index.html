AI/ML for Security research

765 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Artificial intelligence and machine-learning research applies computational models to security questions such as classification, detection and information extraction. A model’s output is meaningful only in relation to its training data, evaluation design and the question it was built to answer.

Scope of this collection

The AI/ML for Security subject assignment supplies this collection’s membership. Papers may be associated with different investigative applications, including threat hunting, phishing analysis and vulnerability assessment. Category membership does not endorse a model or establish readiness for operational use.

Using this research

Compare dataset provenance, train/test separation, class balance, error rates and evaluation dates. Consider whether the test conditions resemble the intended investigation. Treat model-generated text or labels as outputs requiring verification against source evidence rather than as independent evidence of an event.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
