Cyber Threat Intelligence research

761 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Cyber threat intelligence connects observations about threats with the context needed to use them. Research in this area examines how information is collected, extracted, represented and shared, and how an analyst can assess its relevance to a particular question.

Scope of this collection

The bibliography contains papers assigned to Cyber Threat Intelligence in ALEX’s subject taxonomy. It spans intelligence-oriented information processing and analysis; assignment to this category is separate from a paper’s use in threat hunting, attribution or incident response.

Using this research

Use the references to examine an intelligence workflow or the assumptions behind an extracted indicator. Check the provenance, time period and validation of the underlying observations. Published research does not establish that a listed indicator remains current or that a reported association identifies the same actor in another case.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
