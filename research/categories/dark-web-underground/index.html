Dark Web & Underground research

41 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Research on underground online activity examines how forums, markets and concealed services are organised and observed. Investigators may use this literature to understand a setting, interpret terminology or evaluate the limitations of a collected dataset.

Scope of this collection

This subject bibliography follows the existing Dark Web & Underground category assignment. It includes research associated with online markets, underground communities and information collected from those environments. Dark Web Analysis is the separate investigation-type collection for papers assigned to that application.

Using this research

Check when and where observations were collected, how access was obtained and which parts of a community were visible. Platform closures, restricted membership and changing aliases can limit comparisons across datasets. Claims about an account or marketplace should be assessed against the study’s own evidence.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
