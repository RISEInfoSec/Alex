Disinformation & Influence research

175 papers · Research category

Go to the bibliography

Research on disinformation and influence examines how information is produced, circulated and interpreted, including attempts to manipulate an audience. An observed narrative, a pattern of coordination and evidence of an organiser’s intent require different kinds of support.

Scope of this collection

The collection follows the Disinformation & Influence subject assignment in the accepted corpus. Papers can address content analysis, information diffusion and approaches to identifying misleading material. Inclusion does not certify a particular claim, account or political interpretation as false or coordinated.

Using this research

Examine how a study defines its labels, chooses a reference standard and measures uncertainty. Language, collection dates and platform selection can affect the results. Keep content-level observations separate from conclusions about intent, coordination or who is responsible for an activity.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
