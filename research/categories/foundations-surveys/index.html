Foundational work and surveys can help a reader map a research area, understand terminology and identify earlier studies. Their value for an investigation depends on the question being asked and the period and literature that a review covers.

Scope of this collection

This bibliography follows ALEX’s Foundations & Surveys category assignment. It collects records classified as background or overview material rather than selecting papers for a single investigative application. Entries retain the scope and metadata recorded in the accepted library.

Using this research

Start with a review’s stated search strategy, inclusion criteria and publication cutoff. Follow references to primary studies when evaluating a technical or empirical claim. An overview can organise the literature without resolving disagreements between studies or establishing that older techniques remain applicable.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
