A forensic investigation uses digital artefacts to address questions about an event or activity. A reproducible process and a clear account of what the artefacts can show are central to interpreting a technical finding.

Scope of this collection

This application-oriented collection contains records with the Forensic Investigation assignment. Papers may belong to digital forensics, OSINT or other research subjects. It complements the Digital Forensics & Evidence subject bibliography while preserving the distinction between the two classification dimensions.

Using this research

Read how evidence was acquired and preserved, what processing changed its representation and which assumptions support the interpretation. Check tool, software and environment dependencies before attempting reproduction. The research listing provides references, not a determination of evidentiary admissibility or procedural suitability.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
