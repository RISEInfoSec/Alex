Attribution research

45 papers · Investigation type

Go to the bibliography

Attribution research examines how observations may be connected to accounts, systems, groups or people. These are different levels of inference: similarity, shared infrastructure and evidence of control do not necessarily answer the same question.

Scope of this collection

This bibliography includes papers assigned to the Attribution investigation type. Its membership spans the subject categories represented in ALEX. The presence of a paper in this collection is not an endorsement of a particular attribution claim or a statement about an actor’s responsibility.

Using this research

Use the references to examine alternative explanations, uncertainty and the evidence needed for each step in an inference. Check whether apparent links rely on independent sources or repeat the same observation. Report the level of confidence supported by the original evidence rather than extending an association beyond it.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
