Social Media Analysis research

153 papers · Investigation type

Go to the bibliography

Social media analysis examines platform material in relation to an investigative question. Content, account behaviour and interaction patterns offer different observations, and each requires context before an analyst can infer a relationship or activity.

Scope of this collection

The bibliography follows the Social Media Analysis investigation-type assignment. It can draw on several research subjects, including social-media intelligence, information manipulation and machine learning. Subject collections remain available as a separate way to explore the same library.

Using this research

Look at collection boundaries, platform features, language and the way a study handles automated or missing activity. Verify original content where possible and separate an account-level observation from an assertion about a person. Evaluate coordination claims against the study’s definitions and supporting evidence.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
