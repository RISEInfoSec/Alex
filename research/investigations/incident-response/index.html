Incident Response research

108 papers · Investigation type

Go to the bibliography

Incident response brings investigation and action together when a security event needs to be understood and addressed. Research can help explain the information required for a decision and the effects that collection or response actions may have on later analysis.

Scope of this collection

The bibliography follows ALEX’s Incident Response investigation-type assignment. Its records can address different research subjects, from forensic evidence to threat intelligence and network observations. The collection is a set of research references rather than a substitute for an organisation’s incident procedures.

Using this research

Examine what was known when a decision was made, which evidence was preserved and how the study evaluates a response outcome. Separate technical observations from assumptions about an event’s cause or extent. Account for the environment and constraints described by the authors before transferring a proposed process to another setting.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
