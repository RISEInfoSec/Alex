Threat Hunting research

883 papers · Investigation type

Go to the bibliography

Threat hunting starts with a question or hypothesis about possible threat activity and examines available observations for evidence. Research can inform how that question is framed, which signals are examined and how a result is validated.

Scope of this collection

This bibliography uses ALEX’s Threat Hunting investigation-type assignment. It draws on different subjects, including threat intelligence, network security and machine learning. Membership reflects the stored application classification rather than an assertion that a paper describes a complete operational hunting workflow.

Using this research

Compare the data a method requires, the time period it covers and the process used to verify a signal. Distinguish absence of an observation from evidence that an activity did not occur. Evaluate a proposed technique against the visibility and error costs of the investigation in which it might be used.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
