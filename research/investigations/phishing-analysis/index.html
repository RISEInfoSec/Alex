Phishing Analysis research

168 papers · Investigation type

Go to the bibliography

Phishing analysis examines deceptive messages, websites and supporting infrastructure to understand how an attempt works and how related activity might be recognised. Research can help investigators assess indicators and detection methods, while keeping the evidence behind a finding separate from a model’s prediction.

Scope of this collection

This bibliography contains papers assigned to ALEX’s Phishing Analysis investigation type. That assignment describes an investigative application; it is separate from the research category. Papers may therefore approach phishing through fraud research, machine learning, threat intelligence or other subjects represented in the accepted library.

Using this research

Use these references to investigate message and URL characteristics, compare detection approaches and find studies relevant to campaign analysis. Check the original study’s dataset, evaluation period and error rates: results from one dataset or period may not transfer to another investigation. These papers are research references, rather than a live feed of phishing indicators.

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
