The APWG eCrime archive brings together research on electronic crime and its investigation. This ALEX collection provides a publication-year bibliography of the papers carrying that archive’s collection attribution in the accepted library.

Scope of this collection

Membership follows the recorded APWG.org eCrimes Research attribution. The bibliography includes the papers accepted into ALEX, with their existing subject categories and investigation types. Original publication venues and publishers remain part of each paper’s citation, separate from the APWG collection label.

Using this research

Browse the references here or follow the original archive link for the source collection. Use individual DOI and source links to verify citations and consult the original work. Counts reflect accepted ALEX records and update when approved additions are published.

Visit the original collection archive

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
