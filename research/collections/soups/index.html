The Symposium On Usable Privacy and Security (SOUPS) provides a route into research on the human aspects of privacy and security. Its original site links to proceedings across conference years, including material hosted by different publication platforms.

Scope of this collection

This bibliography lists accepted ALEX records with the explicit SOUPS collection attribution. It follows the library’s existing acceptance decisions and retains each paper’s publication metadata, research category and investigation type. The collection label is distinct from the publisher recorded for a particular paper.

Using this research

Follow the original SOUPS archive to navigate conference years, or use the references here to explore the papers available in ALEX. Consult each original publication for its methods, population and findings before applying a result to an investigative question.

Visit the original collection archive

References reproduce the metadata held by ALEX. Missing author, year or source details are identified in the listing. Publication years are shown as recorded; collection and retrieval dates are not substituted. Follow the original source to verify a citation.
