Cybercrime & OSINT research collections

Browse subject and investigation bibliographies from the ALEX research library. Research categories describe a paper’s subject; investigation types describe its investigative application. A paper can appear in both dimensions.

These collections provide publication references and original source links without requiring JavaScript. For full abstracts, keyword searches and all available classifications, search the complete library.

Research category collections

  • OSINT Methodology — 73 papers

    This collection contains papers assigned to ALEX’s OSINT Methodology research category. It brings together work on information collection, validation, linkage and the organisation of investigative work. It is a subject collection, rather than a list of papers selected for one particular kind of investigation.

Investigation type collections

  • Phishing Analysis — 156 papers

    This bibliography contains papers assigned to ALEX’s Phishing Analysis investigation type. That assignment describes an investigative application; it is separate from the research category. Papers may therefore approach phishing through fraud research, machine learning, threat intelligence or other subjects represented in the accepted library.
